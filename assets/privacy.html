Privacy Policy

Effective date: Loading...

WAIV (“WAIV,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides a radio-style music listening experience built around your Apple Music library and listening context. This Privacy Policy explains what information WAIV accesses, how we use it, what we do not do with it, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • the WAIV iOS app
  • the WAIV website
  • WAIV support and contact requests

2. Information WAIV accesses or receives

WAIV is designed to work with your Apple Music account. To provide the app’s core experience, WAIV may access or receive:

  • your Apple Music library availability and catalog context
  • track, artist, album, and playback context needed to generate stations, DJ commentary, and show sequencing
  • app state and usage data needed to provide core functionality, such as free-tier limits, premium entitlement state, and playback/session continuity
  • information you voluntarily provide through the support/contact form, such as your email address and message

WAIV does not collect or store your Apple ID credentials.

3. How we use information

We use information only as needed to operate and improve WAIV’s core functionality, including to:

  • connect to Apple Music and play music through Apple’s services
  • generate DJ-style intros, transitions, and show flow based on your listening context
  • maintain session continuity and app functionality on your device
  • determine whether premium features are unlocked
  • respond to support requests and subscription questions
  • detect, prevent, or troubleshoot bugs and service issues

4. What we do not do

WAIV does not:

  • sell your personal information
  • rent your information to advertisers or data brokers
  • use your data for third-party behavioral advertising
  • create advertising profiles based on your music activity
  • ask for or store your payment card information

Payments for subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple, not WAIV.

5. Apple Music and Apple services

WAIV relies on Apple Music access to function. We use Apple Music-related data only to provide the in-app listening experience.

Apple handles your Apple ID, Apple Music account credentials, billing information, and App Store transaction processing under Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

6. Subscriptions and purchases

If you purchase WAIV Premium, Apple provides us with transaction and entitlement information necessary to confirm whether premium access is active.

WAIV does not receive or store your full payment card details.

7. Analytics, tracking, and advertising

WAIV does not use third-party ad trackers for behavioral advertising.

If we introduce analytics or tracking tools in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so and, where required, request any necessary permissions or consent.

8. Data storage and retention

WAIV tries to minimize stored data.

  • On-device data: certain session, playback, and preference data may be stored locally on your device to make the app function properly.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, we may retain your email address and message for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain support records.
  • Server-side operational data: limited service logs or request data may be retained for security, reliability, fraud prevention, debugging, and service maintenance, then deleted or de-identified when no longer needed.

We do not keep personal information longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Your choices

You can:

  • disconnect or stop using Apple Music with WAIV by revoking access in Apple settings
  • stop future data access by deleting the app
  • cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID / App Store subscription settings
  • contact us to request deletion of support emails or messages you have sent us, subject to legal or operational retention needs

10. Children’s privacy

WAIV is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of information you submitted through support, contact us through the WAIV support page or at https://waiv.fm.
